Palav (Plov)

Serves: 4 · Prep: 20 min · Cook: 1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice (washed and soaked 30 min)
  • 1 lb (450g) lamb or beef, cut into chunks
  • 2 large carrots, julienned
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 cups water or stock
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp coriander, salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: garlic cloves, raisins, barberries

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a heavy pot. Brown the meat until sealed, then remove briefly.
  2. Sauté onions until golden, add carrots and cook until softened.
  3. Return meat to the pot, add spices and a little water, simmer 15–20 minutes.
  4. Drain rice and spread evenly over the meat and vegetables. Do not stir.
  5. Pour hot water or stock to cover rice by about 1 cm; season. Bring to a gentle boil.
  6. Reduce heat, cover tightly and cook 20–30 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed.
  7. Let rest 10 minutes, then gently fluff and serve.

Tip: Toast spices for extra aroma. Serve with fresh herbs and a salad.
